## Toronto skyline

- Source: https://unsplash.com/photos/cn-tower-grayscale-photography-trq3hS53NYU
- Local original: `public/images/toronto-skyline.jpg`
- Responsive derivatives: `public/images/toronto-skyline-960.webp` and `public/images/toronto-skyline-1600.webp`

The photographer is credited beside the image in the rendered page.
